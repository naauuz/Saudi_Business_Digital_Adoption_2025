### KPI interpretation
Executive Overview KPI cards use `AVERAGE()` across the 18 economic activities.

Therefore, the cards represent **simple unweighted activity averages**, not necessarily the official publication Total row.

| Metric | Dashboard simple average | Official Total row |
|---|---:|---:|
| Online Selling % | 33.5 | 33.5 |
| Online Purchasing % | 50.6 | 47.6 |
| Cloud Computing % | 57.5 | 51.3 |
| Avg IoT Usage % | 49.6 | — |

The difference is expected because an official Total may reflect establishment-level aggregation or weighting, while the dashboard treats each economic activity equally.

### Why use the simple average?
The dashboard is designed primarily for **cross-activity comparison and sector profiling**, rather than estimating a weighted national rate.
